# Limitations

- Four questions need wording revision before research use: `g02`, `g10`, `s06`, and `m03`; see [the readiness report](../PROJECT_READINESS_REPORT.md).
- Follow-ups are only sent after initially correct answers, so this protocol measures answer retention and incorrect-suggestion adoption, not true correction of an initially wrong answer.
- The dataset is small, multiple-choice, and mostly straightforward.
- Models, provider APIs, and outputs may change; results are exploratory and do not establish general AI safety or overall reliability.
- The current dataset contains 50 questions across a limited set of subjects; small samples produce substantial statistical uncertainty.
- Mock-provider results are simulated and are not real model findings.
- Repeated model responses may vary, and answer changes alone do not establish sycophancy.
- Initial accuracy determines which questions receive follow-up challenges, so conditional results are not unconditional population estimates.
- Findings from this dataset should not automatically generalize to all models or domains.
